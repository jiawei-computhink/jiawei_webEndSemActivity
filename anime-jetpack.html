Starbound Sprint

A neon anime sky-run through the starlit city.

Hold SPACE to fire your jetpack and rise. Let go to fall. Dodge every drone and reach 1,000 m to win. Touching a drone or the top or bottom of the flight lane ends the run.

Keyboard: hold SPACE to fly · ENTER or R to start/restart · You can also hold the button above.
